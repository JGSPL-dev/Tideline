Your feeds

Paste a site like theguardian.com, a feed link, or a YouTube channel as @name, a channel link, or any video from that channel. Tideline finds the feed for you.

    Get more than 10 stories per feed

    The free feed service returns 10 stories per feed unless you give it a key. Make a free account at rss2json.com, copy your API key from its dashboard, and paste it here. Tideline will then ask for up to 100 stories per feed.

    Advanced: use your own fetch proxy

    Tideline reads feeds through free public proxies, which can be slow or go down. If you run your own, paste its prefix here. The feed address is added to the end.
